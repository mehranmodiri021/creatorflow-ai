## 2. Brand Identity & Visual Assets

### Visual Concept
- **Concept:** Dynamic flow of creator production fused with an inspiration spark and abstract C/F monogram.
- **Style:** High-end 2026 mobile app design, clean vector geometry, smooth 3D bevel lighting, ultra-sharp resolution.

### Brand Color Palette
- **Obsidian Dark Canvas (`#090A0F`):** Deep, distraction-free creative workspace.
- **Electric AI Violet (`#8B5CF6`):** Represents creative intuition, viral hooks, and AI intelligence.
- **Creator Flow Cobalt (`#2563EB`):** Represents video production, structure, and execution.
- **Radiant Gold (`#F59E0B`):** Highlights VIP Unlimited membership, high-retention metrics, and quality badges.
- **Glassmorphic Slate (`#131622`):** Elevated card surfaces and navigation controls.

### App Visual Assets Generated
- **1. Master Launcher Icon:** 1024x1024 master icon (`public/icon.jpg`).
- **2. Android Adaptive Icon:** Foreground vector layer (`ic_launcher_foreground.xml`) and background layer (`ic_launcher_background.xml`) supporting circle, squircle, and rounded-corner masks.
- **3. Splash Screen Design:** 9:16 mobile wallpaper (`public/splash.jpg`).
- **4. Brand Color System:** Defined in Compose `Color.kt` and Tailwind `index.css`.
- **5. App Store Visual Concept:** 16:9 Google Play Feature Graphic banner (`public/feature-graphic.jpg`).
